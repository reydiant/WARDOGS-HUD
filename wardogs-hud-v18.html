    WARDOGS HUD

    Tracks your profile from WARDOGS Tracker. Sign in with Steam on wardogstracker.gg first so your stats sync, and keep your profile public.

    Your SteamID64 is the 17-digit number in your wardogstracker.gg profile URL. Discord lookup only works if you allowed it on the site.
    Kills, deaths, headshots and longest kill only exist for community servers with their kill feed connected. Leave blank if you play official servers.
    Only needed for the Claude coaching tab. Saved in this browser on this PC and sent only to Anthropic. Each breakdown uses a small amount of API credit. Without a key, use Copy for Claude and paste it into the Claude app.
    Controls how often the HUD captures and reads your screen. Pick Low impact if your game FPS drops.
    Needs the kill feed reader and an API key. After each death Claude looks at 5 frames from your screen around the death and explains what most likely killed you. The frames are sent to Anthropic. Capped at 40 reviews per session.
    Drawn as a line on the K/D history chart and used for the Coach goal.
    Pops up when you take 3 deaths for 1 kill or fewer within 8 minutes, so you can change role, loadout or spot.

    Kill feed reader

    This is a frame from the screen you shared. Drag a box around the kill feed, including the team icons at both ends of each line. Then switch to Team score box and drag a box around the three team scores under the minimap (the numbers that race to 100). Last, pick Cash box and drag around your money in the top-right corner (the match total and wallet, plus the small reward lines like "KILL +$1,350" under them). Optional: Kill confirm box around the area just under your crosshair where "KILL CONFIRMED" and "86M // NAME" appear, so kills the feed misses still count. Draw it tall enough to include the lines below them, like "TEAM KILL -$1,800", so team kill fines are read too.

    Drawing
    Leave out your clan tag. Add other names you play under, separated by commas.
    Looser matching catches more misread names but may count the wrong line.
    Words from feed lines that aren't kills, like revives or captures. Separate with commas. If a line with your name gets counted that isn't a kill or death, add a word from it here.
    The HUD compares the small team icons at each end of a kill feed line (same color = same team), so make sure the box includes both icons. This color is only a fallback if the icons can't be read.
    A line containing any of these, or "TK", counts as a team kill.
    The score box is a tiny area read every few seconds: the first team to 100 wins. A short full-screen check runs only if the box disappears with a team at 97+. Whole-screen scanning costs more CPU.
    Only needed if the game names teams instead of showing the words Green, Blue and Red.
    Records your screen while capture is on, keeps the last 90 seconds, and saves the moments around each kill streak worth posting. Turn this off if your game stutters.
    System audio includes everything your PC plays, like Discord voice and music. Changes to audio apply the next time you start capture.
